A migration created an urgent backlog of corrective work. I extended an earlier behavioral-analysis harness into an AI-assisted implementation workflow while completing the tickets themselves.

## Start sequentially, then earn concurrency

Each ticket had an isolated Git worktree and test context. I began with sequential execution to establish a working path, then enabled concurrency. Required validation, including shadow-diff integration tests, had to pass before completion.

## Make failure recoverable

Bounded retries handled transient failures. Persistent failures went to human handoff, with traces, artifacts, and transcripts available for investigation. Human intervention averaged approximately two hours per ticket.

## What changed my approach

During delivery, I replaced prompt-based structured-output enforcement with the available deterministic interface and replaced rigid keyword scope checks with judgment about intent. Later work moved toward a thinner harness: enforce genuine hard requirements and validate outcomes, while leaving ambiguous interpretation to agents.
